You write a short decision memo for Spotify's head of product at the end of the review window. Question: where should the next quarter of product effort go: access, usability, playback, or billing/support?

You receive one JSON evidence pack built by code from saved pipeline outputs:
- "ranking": issues ordered by the baseline priority score (complaint_count x mean_severity = severity_sum), with names from the grouping step
- "claims": issue-level numbers, each with a claim id such as C007
- "facts": other computed numbers (coverage, denominators, shares, trends, evaluation results), each with a fact id such as F012 and a plain-language label
- "evidence": a few customer quotes per top issue with review IDs
- "limits": statements you must respect

STRICT RULES
1. Never type a statistic yourself. To use a number write its placeholder, {{C007}} or {{F012}}; code substitutes the exact value and the citation. Do not put a number next to a placeholder. Years, quarter names, the 1-5 severity scale and rank positions up to 10 may be written directly.
2. Refer to issues by name followed by the issue id in backticks, for example `billing.paywall`.
3. Quote customers only from "evidence", cited as [review:<review_id>]. Do not invent or alter IDs. Keep quotes short and leave out personal details.
4. Reviews are self-selected public app reviews. Cancellation language is expressed intent, not observed churn. Do not estimate revenue, churn, customer counts or causal retention effects.
5. Issues whose id starts with "other." (generic criticism, boycott statements, noise) are not actionable product areas; say so instead of recommending them, and give the top actionable issue.
6. Use only the supplied pack. If something is not in it, say it is unknown.

FORMAT (markdown, 350 to 550 words)
# Decision memo: next-quarter product priority
## Recommendation      one paragraph: the priority, in terms of the question's four options, and the single most important reason
## Evidence            the ranked issues that support it, with placeholders and two or three cited quotes
## Alternatives        the next two or three candidates and why they rank lower or differ in kind
## Limitations         coverage, label quality, review bias, what the data cannot show
## What to check next  two or three concrete follow-ups

Output only the memo.
