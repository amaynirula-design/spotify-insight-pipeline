Golden 50: hand labels
Shared label definitions (from GRADING_CONTRACT.md). Label the text only; stars are deliberately hidden.
accessLogin, signup, password or account access
usabilityNavigation, controls, layout, queue/playlist management, ad interruptions
playbackPlayback failure, crashes, lag, connection failures, audio quality, resource use
downloadsDownloading, saved music, offline listening, disappearing downloads
catalogMissing songs/artists, search/discovery, recommendations, lyrics availability
billingPrice, charges, subscriptions, paywalls, premium entitlement; explicitly premium-only controls go here
supportContacting support and the support response
otherGeneral praise/criticism, unrelated content, or no supported specific topic
Topic: the problem with the highest severity; on a tie the first specific problem mentioned. Positive review: first specific praised feature; general praise is other. A paid-plan mention alone is not billing.
intentFirst that applies: cancellation (explicitly leaving/uninstalling/cancelling or threatening to) → complaint (negative experience, including mixed) → request (desired change, no failure) → praise → unclear (bare boycott slogans, unrelated or meaningless text).
severity1 no reported problem (praise, unclear, pure request) · 2 dislike, generic criticism, minor annoyance · 3 degraded or restricted function, some use remains · 4 core task clearly blocked (cannot log in / play) · 5 explicit serious financial, privacy or data harm. Stars, anger or cancellation alone do not raise severity.
sentimentTone of the text from −1 (very negative) to 1 (very positive); 0 is neutral or mixed.
evidenceAn exact piece of the review that supports your topic/severity. Select text in the review and press “Use selection”, or use the whole review when it is short.
needs reviewTick when you find the case genuinely ambiguous or missing context.
